The tiles in this folder are all CC0 1.0 (public domain), packed into one sheet a set for
hx-multianim's isometric and hex tile map tests (test/examples/168-tilemapGrids and 169-tilemapLifted).

isoCity.png / isoCity.atlas2
  Kenney, "Isometric Tiles City" (cityTiles 066, 072, 074, 080, 073, 081, 089, 067) and
  "Isometric Tiles Buildings" (buildingTiles 002). https://kenney.nl/assets/isometric-tiles-city,
  https://kenney.nl/assets/isometric-tiles-buildings. License: CC0 1.0. Credit (Kenney or
  www.kenney.nl) is welcome, not required.

hexKenney.png / hexKenney.atlas2
  Kenney, "Hexagon Pack" (Tiles/Terrain: grass_05, grass_06, dirt_06, sand_07, stone_05).
  https://kenney.nl/assets/hexagon-pack. License: CC0 1.0.

hexset.png / hexset.atlas2
  mbartelsm, "Hexset v0.1.1" (temperate, deep_water, desert, stone_mountain, temperate_hill).
  https://opengameart.org/content/hexset-v011-hex-pixel-art-terrain-tileset. License: CC0 1.0.

padded.png / padded.atlas2 / paddedTrimmed.atlas2
  Four of isoCity.png's tiles and two of hexset.png's (above), each with clear margins around it,
  and a figure drawn for these tests: padded.atlas2 lists the frames whole, paddedTrimmed.atlas2
  the same frames trimmed to their pixels (TileGridTest's trimmed atlas test). CC0 1.0, as above.

lifted.png / lifted.atlas2
  Blocks, side pieces, faces, an edge, a roof, a hall and figures drawn for these tests
  (test/examples/169-tilemapLifted), dedicated to the public domain: CC0 1.0.

CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
